WebSnip

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to the WebSnip app for Android (com.websnip.studio)

WebSnip doesn't collect any of your data.

Privacy

Nothing is collected

WebSnip has no accounts, analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking. The app never sends the developer any information about you or how you use it.

What stays on your phone

The web addresses you save, the position of each frame, widget names, the captured pictures and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They're deleted when you delete a widget in the app or remove it from your home screen, and when you uninstall the app.

Websites you open

Pages load straight from the websites you choose, the same way a browser loads them. Those sites can set cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies. WebSnip keeps their cookies on your phone so you stay signed in and your choices on those sites are remembered.

Internet use

WebSnip connects to the internet only to load the pages you choose, including when a widget refreshes.

Tip payments

Tips are optional and handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Emails to the developer

If you write for support, your email address and message are used only to answer you. Support emails include your app version and phone model, which you can see and remove before sending.

Children

WebSnip isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and online, with a new date.

Terms of use

Your use

WebSnip shows parts of websites you choose, for your own personal use. The content belongs to those websites, so please respect their terms.

As is

WebSnip is provided as is, without warranty. The developer isn't responsible for the content of websites, or for refreshes that don't work when a site changes or is unavailable.

Tips

Tips are voluntary gifts to support development. They don't unlock anything. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies.
